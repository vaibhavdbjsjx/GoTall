Common questions

Where is my data? Only on your iPhone. Linden has no accounts and no server.

How do I restore Premium? Profile › Restore purchases, signed in with the Apple Account that bought it.

How do I cancel? Settings › Apple Account › Subscriptions on your iPhone, or Profile › Manage subscription in the app.

Why are Ask Linden answers short or from the guide? Conversational answers need iOS 26 with Apple Intelligence turned on. Other iPhones get answers from Linden's built-in guide.

Is the adult-height estimate a prediction? No. It shows where height would land if growth stays in its current percentile channel on the CDC chart. It's not a guarantee.

How do I delete everything? Profile › Privacy & data › Delete all data.
