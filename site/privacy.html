Privacy Policy

Effective 18 October 2026

In short

Linden keeps your growth data on your iPhone. We, the developer, don't collect, receive, sell or share personal data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

What the app stores on your device

Profiles you create: an optional nickname, birth date, the growth chart used (female or male), height measurements, optional parents' heights, habit check-ins and your preferences.

This data is saved on your device with iOS data protection. It is not sent to us or to any server we run; Linden has no server.

Ask Linden

Questions are answered on your device, using Apple's on-device model where available or Linden's built-in guide. Your questions and the answers are not sent to us or to anyone else, and conversations are not saved after you close them. The app only keeps a count of questions asked each day.

Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Apple. We don't receive your name, email or payment details. The app checks your subscription status on your device using Apple's StoreKit. Apple's privacy policy applies to purchases.

Reminders, reports and exports

Reminders are scheduled on your device. Reports (PDF) and data exports (JSON) are created on your device and are only shared if you choose to share them.

Children

Linden is designed for parents and caregivers to track a child's growth, and for teenagers and adults to track their own. Profiles for children under 13 are created and managed by a parent. Because we collect no personal information from anyone, we collect none from children.

Backups and deleting your data

You can delete everything at any time in Profile › Privacy & data › Delete all data. Deleting the app also removes its data from the device. If you use iCloud Backup, Apple may include app data in your device backup according to your iCloud settings.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will appear in the app and on our website with a new effective date. Questions: see the support page linked in the app and on the App Store.
