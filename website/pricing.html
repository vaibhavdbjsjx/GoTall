Billing questions

How does the free trial work?
The yearly plan starts with 3 days free. If you don't cancel at least 24 hours before the trial ends, it converts to a paid subscription at the price shown.
How do I cancel?
Go to Settings › Apple Account › Subscriptions on your iPhone, or Profile › Manage subscription in the app. You keep Pro until the end of the period you paid for. Deleting the app, its data or your account doesn't cancel a subscription. The lifetime purchase never renews.
Does one purchase cover my family?
Yes. Gromax Pro supports Family Sharing, so up to five family members can use it at no extra cost.
Can I get a refund?
Purchases are handled by Apple, so refunds are too. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.
How do I restore Gromax Pro?
Profile › Restore purchases, signed in with the Apple Account that bought it. Still stuck? Contact support.
