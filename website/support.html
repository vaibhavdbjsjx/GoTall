FAQ

Common questions

Where is my data?
On your iPhone. If you sign in (optional), a backup is kept with your account so you can restore it on a new iPhone.
Can routines make me taller?
No exercise changes bone length. Routines help posture, mobility and core strength, which can help you stand at your full height.
Is the adult-height estimate a prediction?
It shows where height would land if growth stays in its current percentile channel on the CDC chart. It's not a guarantee.
Is the posture check recorded?
No. The camera image is analysed on your iPhone and discarded; only the score is kept.
Why are Gromax Coach answers short or from the guide?
Conversational answers need Apple Intelligence on a supported iPhone. Other iPhones get answers from Gromax's built-in guide.
How do I restore Gromax Pro?
Profile › Restore purchases, signed in with the Apple Account that bought it.
How do I cancel?
Settings › Apple Account › Subscriptions on your iPhone, or Profile › Manage subscription in the app. The lifetime purchase never renews.
How do I delete everything?
Profile › Privacy & data › Delete all data. To delete your account and backup: Profile › Account › Delete account.
