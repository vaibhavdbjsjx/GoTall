Why Gromax

Growth, measured honestly.

Height apps love big promises. Gromax shows you where you really are on the growth chart, gives an estimate with its reasoning, and builds the everyday habits that help you stand at your full height. It's also useful for the practical things growing kids bring, like clothes that suddenly don't fit.

Posture check

See your posture the way others do.

Stand in front of your iPhone for 30 seconds, from the front or the side. Gromax scores head, shoulder and hip alignment and picks routines for what it finds. It's analysed on your iPhone, and no photo or video is saved or sent.

Head forward → aligned
Shoulders tilted → level

Real growth charts

Height percentiles from the CDC growth charts, explained in plain words, with growth speed read the way clinicians read it.

An honest estimate

Where adult height would land if growth stays on its percentile line. A range with its reasoning, never a guarantee.

Guided routines

Posture, mobility, core, morning, evening and desk-break routines with a timer, cues and haptics.

A daily plan

One routine, your habits, food variety and bedtime. Levels, points and a forgiving streak that never shames a missed day.

Gromax Coach

Ask anything. It stays on your iPhone.

Questions about growth, sleep, food, posture or measuring are answered on your device, using your own data. No invented numbers and no "grow taller" tricks.
