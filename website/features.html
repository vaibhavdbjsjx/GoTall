Gromax Coach

Answers in seconds, made on your iPhone.

Ask about growth, sleep, food, posture or measuring. Answers use your own data and are generated on your device with Apple's on-device model. iPhones without Apple Intelligence get answers from Gromax's built-in guide.

  • Grounded in your own measurements
  • No invented numbers, no medical claims
  • Nothing is sent to a server
  • Conversations aren't saved after you close them
How should I measure my height at home?
Barefoot, heels against a wall, looking straight ahead. Measure at the same time of day each time, since you're slightly taller in the morning.
Will stretching make me taller?
No exercise changes bone length. Routines help posture and mobility, which can help you stand at your full height.
