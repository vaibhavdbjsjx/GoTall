### Recommendation: **B. US first (US App Store storefront only)**

Reasons: (1) the scientifically appropriate reference (CDC 2000) is the one we can legally ship today. (2) It has the highest willingness to pay among parents. (3) One legal regime to get right first. (4) English only. India is attractive for **phase 2 expansion** once IAP 2015 (or WHO) permission is obtained and the DPDP verifiable-parental-consent flow is designed. A local-first design (no server-side personal data) may simplify DPDP, but **counsel must confirm**.

*Why not global:* shipping CDC percentiles to non-US teens would label many healthy children as "low percentile" and trigger false signposts. That would be a scientific and reputational failure.

**Expansion order (proposal):** US → Canada/Australia/NZ (after a reference decision. Canada uses WHO-based charts → needs WHO permission) → India (IAP licence + DPDP) → UK/EU (UK-WHO licence, GDPR/AADC).

## 2. Positioning

**1-line:**
> The honest way to track a child's growth: real growth charts, a transparent adult-height range, and healthy habits without hype.

**3-line App Store positioning (draft, no medical claims):**
> See where growth is heading with a transparent adult-height range based on CDC growth charts.
> Log measurements, watch percentiles and growth rate over time, and share a clear report with your doctor.
> Private by design: your family's data stays on your devices.

**5 core product principles**
1. **Honesty over hype:** ranges, sources and limits are always visible.
2. **Value before payment:** the estimate and the basic chart are free.
3. **Safety for young people:** parent-managed under-13 profiles, no body photos, no calorie counting, signposting to doctors.
4. **Privacy by default:** local-first, no tracking, delete everything in two taps.
5. **Long-term usefulness:** built for years of measuring, not a one-time quiz.

**5 things we explicitly refuse to do**
1. Claim that any exercise, food, sleep routine or supplement adds centimetres or "boosts HGH".
2. Hide a result behind a paywall after a long questionnaire, or use fake countdowns and urgency.
3. Ask for ethnicity, body photos or other data we don't need.
4. Present an exact future height, accuracy percentages or "AI knows your height".
5. Track users across apps or sell or share data for advertising.

**5 major differentiators**
1. Free, transparent adult-height range with a public Methods page.
2. Real CDC percentile charts and growth rate, the same frame of reference pediatricians use in the US.
3. Family profiles + a doctor-ready PDF report.
4. Privacy-first local storage with no tracking SDKs.
5. Gentle safety signposting ("worth mentioning to your doctor") instead of fear-based marketing.
